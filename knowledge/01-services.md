# Services

## AI voice agents

Inbound and outbound voice agents that answer questions, qualify callers and
capture their details. They can run on a website or on a phone number. This
demo you are speaking to now is an example of one.

## AI chat agents and assistants

Website chat agents, WhatsApp assistants and internal assistants that answer
from a company's own documents and hand off to a human when needed.

## Business automation

End-to-end workflows built in n8n and similar tools: form to CRM, invoice
chasing, onboarding sequences, report generation, data entry between systems
that were never designed to talk to each other.

## Lead generation systems

B2B lead sourcing, enrichment and personalised outreach at scale. Apollo and
similar sources feed an enrichment step, then messages are personalised per
prospect rather than blasted from one template.

## Content pipelines

Automated content production: research, drafting, repurposing one piece into
many formats, scheduling and publishing. Includes avatar and voice content
where that fits the brand.

## Training and consulting

One-to-one and group training for owners and teams who want to build and
maintain these systems themselves.

## Tools we work with

n8n, Claude and other large language models, AssemblyAI, Fish Audio, HeyGen,
Apollo.io, Google Workspace, Airtable, Supabase, and self-hosted infrastructure
via Coolify and Docker.
