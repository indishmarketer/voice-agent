## How we price

We price per project rather than per hour, so the cost is known before we
start. Ongoing systems can also be run on a monthly retainer that covers
hosting, monitoring and changes.

## What to say on a call

Do not quote a specific figure on the call. Say that pricing depends on how
many systems are involved and how much data has to move, and that the team
will send exact numbers by email once they understand the process.

## Discovery call

The first conversation is free. It is a short call to understand the process
and confirm whether automation is worth it for that business.
