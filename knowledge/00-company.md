# Indish Marketer — Company

## Who we are

Indish Marketer is an AI automation studio and educational platform. We build
AI agents and automations that take repetitive manual work off a business, and
we teach business owners how these systems work so they are not dependent on
anyone.

Website: indishmarketer.com

## Who we help

- Small businesses drowning in manual admin and follow-up
- Coaches and consultants who lose leads because they cannot reply fast enough
- Creators and agencies who need content and outreach running without them
- Local service businesses that miss calls and enquiries

## How we are different

- We self-host and use open tooling wherever possible, so our clients keep
  their monthly software costs very low.
- We build systems the owner actually understands, rather than a black box.
- We start with the one process that is costing the most time, prove it works,
  then expand.

## What a first conversation looks like

We ask what the business does, which task eats the most time each week, and
what happens today when a lead comes in. From that we can usually say within a
few minutes whether automation will pay for itself.
